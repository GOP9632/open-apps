(01) The shop

A café where you paint your own picture.

Gray Room sits in a thirty-year-old house, a few minutes from Chung Yuan Christian University. Everything inside is gray on purpose: concrete walls, a plastered arch, black track lights. The only color in the room is what the guests paint.
